# Mandatory Quality Gates

A single critical gate failure prevents `PROJECT_COMPLETE`.

## G1 — Architecture

Pass when the seven cores are construct-distinct, collectively sufficient for the defined project scope, and major overlaps are explicitly modeled.

## G2 — Coverage

Pass when no known critical domain required for the defined copywriting scope remains absent.

## G3 — Evidence

Pass when material scientific claims preserve source provenance, design/context, evidence status and uncertainty.

## G4 — Causal Discipline

Pass when correlation, prediction, mechanism, quasi-causality and experimental causality remain distinguishable.

## G5 — Connection Validity

Pass when critical graph edges contain mechanism/evidence or explicit provisional/insufficient-evidence status.

## G6 — Contradiction Integrity

Pass when negative, null and contradictory evidence are retained and affect confidence/boundaries.

## G7 — Measurement Validity

Pass when observable proxies are not silently treated as latent psychological constructs and alternative explanations are preserved.

## G8 — Reasoning

Pass when the engine can reason across multiple cores, surface tensions, retrieve relevant support and propagate uncertainty appropriately.

## G9 — Copy Quality

Pass when outputs demonstrate context-sensitive strategy rather than formula/template imitation and satisfy factual/brand constraints.

## G10 — Generalization

Pass when representative holdout tasks across audiences, products, channels and awareness states remain acceptable.

## G11 — Feedback Learning

Pass when observed data can update bounded runtime hypotheses/calibration without naively rewriting scientific knowledge.

## G12 — Skill Quality

Pass when triggering, retrieval, runtime workflow, boundaries, failure handling and outputs meet the reusable Skill acceptance criteria.

## Internal "100/100"

Internal 100/100 means:

- all 12 mandatory gates PASS;
- all required outputs exist;
- all dependencies are resolved;
- no known unresolved critical gap remains;
- release evidence is persisted.

It does not mean universal scientific completeness or perfect prediction of human behavior.

# Copywriting Reasoning Engine

The engine converts grounded S5 commercial diagnosis into an evidence-aware subgraph, mechanism hypotheses, conflicts, message strategy, generation constraints and critique.

## Pipeline

1. INGEST_CONTEXT
2. VALIDATE_FACTS_AND_CONSTRAINTS
3. LOAD_S5_DIAGNOSIS
4. PLAN_RETRIEVAL
5. RETRIEVE_RELEVANT_SUBGRAPH
6. BUILD_ACTIVATION_LEDGER
7. DETECT_MECHANISM_AND_DESIGN_CONFLICTS
8. PROPAGATE_UNCERTAINTY
9. SYNTHESIZE_VALUE_AND_BELIEF_MODEL
10. FORM_MESSAGE_HYPOTHESIS
11. BUILD_CLAIM_PROOF_FRAMING_PLAN
12. BUILD_OBJECTION_SEQUENCE_LANGUAGE_CTA_PLAN
13. ADAPT_TO_CHANNEL_AND_BRAND
14. GENERATE_COPY_WITH_FACT_LOCK
15. CRITIQUE_AGAINST_STRATEGY_EVIDENCE_AND_CONSTRAINTS
16. EMIT_EXPERIMENT_HOOKS_FOR_UNCERTAIN_DECISIONS

## Invariants

- No copy formula is mandatory.
- Every material strategy recommendation must trace to facts plus a mechanism/design rationale.
- Scientific nodes describe mechanisms; commercial objects describe context.
- Provisional constructs remain provisional.
- No invented product facts, proof, customer quotes, metrics, guarantees, scarcity, deadlines or social proof.
- No numerical runtime parameter may be called an empirical effect size unless it actually is one from a cited study and is used only as evidence metadata.
- A valid outcome may be 'insufficient evidence; ask/research/test' rather than a persuasive claim.

No formula such as AIDA/PAS is mandatory. Formulae may be used later only as optional execution patterns when they fit the diagnosed mechanism and channel.

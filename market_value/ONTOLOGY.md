# Market & Value Outer System

This layer represents commercial reality. It is not an eighth psychological core.

## MV-D01 — Audience & Situation

Who is acting, in what situation, with what knowledge/context.

Examples: segment, role, current state, desired state, knowledge state, awareness state.

Not equivalent to: identity salience, motivation, attitude.

## MV-D02 — Goals, Jobs & Desired Outcomes

What outcome the audience is trying to achieve or avoid.

Examples: job-to-be-done, target outcome, success criterion.

Not equivalent to: active goal representation unless currently evidenced, goal commitment.

## MV-D03 — Problems, Barriers & Costs

What blocks progress or makes current state costly.

Examples: time cost, effort, confusion, social risk, financial cost.

Not equivalent to: perceived motivational cost unless recipient-side evidence exists.

## MV-D04 — Product & Capability

What the product can actually do, how, and under what limitations.

Examples: feature, capability, delivery, limitation.

Not equivalent to: value, benefit, belief.

## MV-D05 — Alternatives & Status Quo

What the audience can choose instead, including doing nothing.

Examples: competitor, DIY, status quo, substitute.

Not equivalent to: reference point until psychologically operative.

## MV-D06 — Value Hypotheses

Candidate ways the product creates value relative to goals, alternatives and sacrifices.

Examples: functional outcome, economic, time/convenience, emotional, identity/social, risk reduction, uncertainty reduction, autonomy/control, effort reduction.

Not equivalent to: subjective task value until receiver-side evidence exists.

## MV-D07 — Offer & Commercial Terms

Concrete exchange structure.

Examples: price, bundle, guarantee, payment terms, eligibility, deadline.

Not equivalent to: scarcity, loss aversion, urgency.

## MV-D08 — Risk, Objections & Belief Gaps

Reasons the audience may reject, delay or doubt.

Examples: trust concern, plausibility concern, fit concern, switching risk, performance risk.

Not equivalent to: reactance, counterarguing, risk perception without diagnosis.

## MV-D09 — Friction & Action Path

Practical burdens between interest/intention and completed action.

Examples: steps, form fields, setup, switching work, delay, unclear next step.

Not equivalent to: low motivation, intention-behavior gap by itself.

## MV-D10 — Proof & Evidence Inventory

Available support for claims and its traceability/diagnosticity.

Examples: demo, data, testimonial, method, case, certification, source.

Not equivalent to: truth, argument quality, trust automatically.

## MV-D11 — Customer Language

Verbatim language, concepts, objections, metaphors and category terms used by the audience.

Examples: quotes, search phrases, support language, sales-call wording.

Not equivalent to: common ground or connotation unless contextualized.

## MV-D12 — Channel, Format & Placement

Communication environment and constraints.

Examples: landing page, email, Reels, ad, sales call, character limit.

Not equivalent to: attention state, elaboration, motivation.

## MV-D13 — Market & Category Context

Competitive/category conventions, sophistication, norms, substitutes and expectations.

Examples: category claims, pricing norms, common proof patterns, market maturity.

Not equivalent to: descriptive norm unless referent and perception are evidenced.

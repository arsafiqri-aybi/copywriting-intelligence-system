# Research Promotion Policy

## Principle

Promotion is a lifecycle decision, not a confidence vibe.

A large number of citations does not automatically create a canonical node or edge.

## Source lifecycle

`candidate → screened_in/screened_out → extracted → verified_for_claim → superseded/retracted`

Retrieval of an abstract is sufficient for **taxonomy discovery** but not automatically sufficient for fine-grained causal claims.

## Module promotion

A module can be taxonomy-accepted when:

1. it represents a coherent construct/research family;
2. its boundary against neighboring modules is explicit;
3. it has at least one credible research tradition or synthesis source;
4. it is operationally useful for retrieval/decomposition;
5. known overlap is modeled rather than hidden.

Taxonomy acceptance does **not** imply all child neurons are accepted.

## Neuron promotion

A candidate neuron may become `accepted` only when:

- its definition is construct-specific;
- inclusion/exclusion boundaries are explicit;
- it is not merely marketing jargon or a duplicate alias;
- supporting scientific claims have traceable sources;
- important contradictory/null evidence has been searched for;
- measurement/operationalization is documented or explicitly absent;
- transport/generalization limits are recorded;
- unresolved uncertainty is below the threshold required for its intended runtime use.

Otherwise use `provisional` or keep under review.

## Scientific edge promotion

An edge must not be promoted because two nodes sound related.

For `accepted` edge status require:

- source and target are valid nodes;
- relation/direction is explicit;
- mechanism is stated;
- at least one supporting claim is verified at the appropriate evidence level;
- causal language matches study design;
- moderators/boundaries are represented when material;
- contradictory/null evidence is considered;
- runtime weight remains separate from empirical effect metadata.

If mechanism is plausible but evidence is incomplete, use `provisional`.
If evidence supports no direct relation, preserve a non-edge decision.

## Measurement promotion

A measurement mapping requires:

- observed variable;
- target construct(s);
- rationale;
- validity evidence when available;
- reliability when available;
- alternative explanations;
- population/context/channel scope;
- calibration status.

Platform metrics are proxies unless direct validity evidence says otherwise.

## Feedback promotion

Campaign observations may update audience/channel/message hypotheses.

They do not automatically rewrite scientific knowledge.

Scientific nodes/edges require evidence appropriate to scientific claims; causal updates require causal identification appropriate to the claim.

# Market & Value Workflow

Use this reference when the task requires audience diagnosis, positioning, value, offer, objection, proof, alternatives, friction, or customer-language interpretation.

## Outer-system rule

Market & Value is commercial context, not an eighth psychological core.

Read selectively:

- `market_value/ontology.json` — commercial dimensions and boundaries.
- `market_value/object-model.json` — value, objection, proof, friction, alternative objects.
- `market_value/mapping-rules.json` — grounded commercial signal → candidate mechanism mappings.
- `market_value/inference-policy.json` — evidence precedence and non-inference rules.
- `market_value/resolver.json` — full diagnosis algorithm.

## Minimal diagnosis

Build only the objects needed for the task:

1. audience situation and target action;
2. current alternative/status quo;
3. desired outcome;
4. product capability relevant to that outcome;
5. value hypothesis relative to alternatives/sacrifices;
6. belief/trust gap;
7. objection/risk;
8. proof available/missing;
9. decision/implementation friction;
10. customer language with provenance.

Every inferred mechanism remains a hypothesis unless evidence supports stronger status.

## Core boundary

Market & Value remains an **outer system**, never an eighth psychological core.

Commercial terms such as audience, offer, pain point, benefit, feature, objection, proof, CTA, awareness state and market sophistication remain commercial/runtime objects unless separate scientific evidence validates a psychological construct.

## Representative negative checks

The test suite explicitly verifies that:

- a price reference does not automatically become loss aversion;
- a desired self does not automatically become status motivation;
- a deadline does not automatically become scarcity/urgency/fear;
- a feature does not automatically become subjective value;
- customer language does not automatically become a latent mental state;
- many options do not automatically become choice overload;
- a guarantee does not automatically prove trust or quality.

## Handoff to S6

S6 receives grounded context, value structure, bounded mechanism hypotheses, conflicts, trust/belief gaps, friction, proof requirements, customer language, non-inferences and open questions.

S6 may reason from these objects. It may not silently promote them into scientific knowledge.

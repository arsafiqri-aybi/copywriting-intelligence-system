# Architecture v1.1 — S1 Taxonomy Revision

This revision supersedes module labels in `ARCHITECTURE_V1.md` while preserving the seven protected top-level cores and stable module IDs.

## Scientific interpretation

The seven cores are organizing containers, **not claims that human cognition is biologically partitioned into seven modules**. Cross-core mechanisms are expected. A node receives one primary membership for retrieval plus secondary memberships when evidence requires them.

## Seven protected cores

1. **C1 — Cognitive Processing & Attention**
2. **C2 — Motivation & Affect**
3. **C3 — Social Psychology & Identity**
4. **C4 — Judgment & Decision**
5. **C5 — Persuasion, Belief & Trust**
6. **C6 — Language, Pragmatics & Meaning**
7. **C7 — Rhetoric, Argumentation & Message Architecture**

## Boundary rules

- C1 processing fluency may affect C5 judgment/persuasion but is not itself persuasion.
- C2 valuation/motivation may mediate C5 persuasion but value is not equivalent to belief.
- C3 social identity/norms can alter C5 source/message reception; trust remains primarily C5.
- C4 framing describes changed representation/evaluation; C7 describes deliberate construction of a frame.
- C5 credibility/trust is an audience judgment; C7 ethos is a rhetorical strategy/category.
- C6 builds interpreted meaning; C7 organizes that meaning into deliberate persuasive structure.
- C7 CTA is message design; actual commitment/intention/behavior processes live in C5/C4.

## Promotion status

S1 validates the **taxonomy containers**, not the scientific truth of all future child neurons. Modules marked provisional in `research/s1/module-lifecycle.json` require deeper construct research before their child nodes can be promoted broadly.

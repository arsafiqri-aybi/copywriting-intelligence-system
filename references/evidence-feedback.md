# Evidence, Measurement & Feedback

Use this reference when making scientific/causal claims, interpreting proof, analyzing metrics, designing experiments, or learning from outcomes.

## Evidence

Follow `research/EVIDENCE_POLICY.md` and `research/PROMOTION_POLICY.md`.

Scientific claim language must match the study/evidence status. Preserve moderators, contradictions, and transport limits.

## Metrics

Read `feedback/observable-metric-catalog.json`.

Key rule: an observed platform/commercial metric is not a direct measure of a latent psychological state unless a construct-specific validation supports that mapping.

Examples:

- impressions ≠ attention;
- video retention ≠ validated sustained attention;
- CTR ≠ persuasion mechanism;
- conversion ≠ theory validation;
- unsubscribe ≠ direct reactance measurement.

## Experiments

Read `feedback/experiment-policy.json` and `feedback/experiment-templates.json`.

A valid randomized experiment may support a causal claim about the randomized intervention in the tested context. A multi-component variant does not identify each component/mechanism.

## Feedback

Read `feedback/update-policy.json`, `feedback/update-engine.json`, and `feedback/lifecycle.json`.

Campaign outcomes update bounded runtime hypotheses first. Scientific knowledge changes only through the research promotion lifecycle.
